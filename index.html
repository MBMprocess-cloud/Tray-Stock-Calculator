The oven doesn't slow down when the packing machines do. Whatever the machines can't take is packed into trays by hand. This works out how many trays per minute.

Tray stock

Machine output = speed × pack weight (g) ÷ 1000 kg/min, added up across running machines.

Trays per minute = (oven output kg/hr ÷ 60 − machine output kg/min) ÷ tray weight (kg). If the machines take more than the oven sends, no trays are needed.

Oven output: PDN-SP-243, issue 02 (30.04.2025). Tray weights: PDN-SP-243, revision 02 (21.11.2023).

Machine speeds and 8-hour case targets: Packetting Machine Targets sheet. Grouped machines on the sheet (such as MP 08/09/10) are shown one by one, each with the sheet speed. A speed range is shown as its low and high end.
